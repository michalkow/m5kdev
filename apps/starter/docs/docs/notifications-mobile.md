---
title: Read notifications on mobile
---

After signing in, choose **Notifications** on the home screen to open your
inbox. Notifications shown there belong to your current organization membership.

To receive notifications on your phone, choose **Enable push for this device**
and allow notifications when your phone asks. If permission was denied, review
the app's notification permission in your phone settings.

Push notifications may not be available on every device. You can still open
the app and read your inbox. Choose **Back to posts** to return to the home
screen.
