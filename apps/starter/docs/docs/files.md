---
title: Upload and open images
---

1. Open **Files** in the web product.
2. Choose **Upload image** and select an image from your device.
3. Wait for the upload confirmation. The image appears in the list.

Choose **Open** beside an image to view it. You must be signed in and have
access to that file. Choose **Delete** to remove a file you no longer need.

If an upload fails, check your connection and try again. An unsuccessful
upload has not been confirmed as available in your file list.
