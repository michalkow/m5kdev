## Create your sign-in

Open the product and choose the sign-up option. Enter the details requested on
the form and submit it. If you receive a verification email, follow its link to
confirm your email address. If sign-up asks for an invitation code, you need a
valid code before you can continue.

Use the link from the verification email without changing its address. If it is
invalid or has expired, request a fresh verification email from the same website.

## Sign in

Enter the email address and password you used when signing up. After a
successful sign-in, the product opens your signed-in home screen.

## Reset a forgotten password

1. On the sign-in screen, choose the forgotten-password option.
2. Enter your email address and submit the form.
3. Check your inbox and spam folder for a password-reset email.
4. Follow the complete link and enter your new password twice.
5. Wait for the success message, then return to sign in with your new password.

If the link is incomplete, invalid, or has expired, request another reset email
from the same website. Keep reset links private and do not edit their address.

## Join an organisation by invitation

Open the complete invitation link supplied by an organisation manager. Keep its
website address and all link details unchanged. If you are signed in with the
invited email address, the product accepts the invitation and opens your home or
member-management screen. If another email address is signed in, choose **Switch
account**, then use the invited address.

If you need a new sign-in, the invitation opens sign-up with the invited email
already filled in. Set a password or choose an offered sign-in provider. Keep the
same website open until sign-up finishes. Accepting the invitation joins the
organisation named on the screen; switching organisations later keeps the current
product website and your saved language preferences.

A cancelled, expired or incomplete link cannot be accepted. Ask the organisation
manager for a fresh invitation from the intended website. Managers can copy a
complete invitation link from the pending member's row on the members screen.

## Use a waitlist invitation

If sign-up shows a waitlist, enter your email address to request access. Check your
inbox for confirmation. When access is offered, open the complete invitation link
and finish sign-up on that website. A friend may also share an invitation link;
use the whole link rather than copying only its code.

An invitation can expire or be used only once. If the screen cannot validate it,
ask for another link. Do not change the website address or remove link details to
try again. If your product offers an invite-friends screen, use its copy button to
share the complete generated link.

## Claim an account prepared for you

An administrator may supply a sign-in link for an account they prepared. Open the
complete link on its original website. The link is short-lived and can sign you
in once; ask the administrator for a new one if it has expired or was already used.
The **Claim your account** screen lets you save your real email address, link an
offered sign-in provider, or set a password with **Set Password and Claim**.

Stay on that website while completing these steps. After successful completion,
the product opens your signed-in home. Changing the account's email makes older
sign-in links unusable; use a fresh link for the new address. If the screen reports
an invalid or expired account link, ask the administrator to generate another.

If the reset request cannot be sent, the form shows an error. Try again before
waiting for an email. A successful request keeps account details private; it does
not confirm whether an email address has an account.
