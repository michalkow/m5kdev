title: Create and manage posts
---

On the web, open **Posts** to write drafts, edit existing posts, and mark them
as published. The posts you can see and change depend on your access in the
selected organization.

## Write a draft

1. Choose **Create post**.
2. Enter a **Title** and **Content**. You can also add a short **Excerpt** and a
   **Slug**, the short name used to identify the post.
3. Choose **Create draft**.

The new post appears with the **Draft** status. To revise it, choose **Edit**,
make your changes, and choose **Save changes**.

## Find and publish a post

Use **Search posts** to find text in a post, or use **Status** to show only
drafts or published posts. Select a post's preview button to read its contents
in the preview panel.

Choose **Publish** on a draft to change its status to **Published**. This does
not create a public reading page or share a link outside the product.

## Remove a post from the list

Choose **Delete**, then confirm with **Archive post**. The post disappears
from the main list. Choose **Keep it** to cancel.
