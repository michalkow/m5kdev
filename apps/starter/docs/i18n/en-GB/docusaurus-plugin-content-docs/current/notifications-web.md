---
title: Manage notifications on the web
---

Open the inbox in the sidebar to read notifications. Open **Notifications** to
change which notification channels you receive for the selected organization.
Other organizations keep their own settings.

To receive browser notifications, choose **Enable push for this device** and
allow notifications when your browser asks. This applies to the browser you
are using. If you previously blocked notifications, change the permission in
your browser's site settings, then try again.

Browser notifications may be unavailable on some devices. You can still read
notifications in the inbox.
